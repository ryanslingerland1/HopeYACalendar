Google Calendar

Set up on a computer, then view on your phone.

Add to Google Calendar (opens in a new tab)
How to subscribe
  1. Open the link on a computer and sign in to your Google account.
  2. Confirm Add when Google Calendar prompts you.
  3. On your phone, open Google Calendar with the same account and make sure this calendar is checked in the menu.

Apple Calendar

For iPhone, iPad, and Mac.

Subscribe in Apple Calendar
How to subscribe
  1. Tap the link on your Apple device and allow it to open Calendar.
  2. Follow the prompts to confirm the subscription.
  3. Choose iCloud if offered to see it across your Apple devices, then save.
Need to add it manually?

Google: On a computer, open Google Calendar. Next to Other calendars, click +, then From URL. Paste the link and click Add calendar.

iPhone or iPad: In Calendar, open Calendars → Add Calendar → Add Subscription Calendar. Paste the link, tap Find (or Subscribe on older versions), then finish saving.

Mac: In Calendar, choose File → New Calendar Subscription, paste the link, and click Subscribe. Choose your settings and click OK.

Updates may take a little time to appear in your calendar.

More help: Google’s subscription guide · Apple’s subscription guide.
